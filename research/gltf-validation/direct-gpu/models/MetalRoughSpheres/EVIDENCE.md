### AO cause and candidate fix

The no-authored-AO source material exposes `g_tAmbientOcclusion` as a declared parameter but its actual texture was invalid/null. `/tmp/metalrough-ao-control-01/summary.json` SHA-256 `bf884b23ddc5f87dfde885a72e762c611241e70ed0059689900f37bf0a1e6ee8` records private same-source material controls. The source AO debug image exhibits the irregular black/white pattern. A 1x1 white AO override on a private copy removes the pattern in both AO-debug and normal-lit images; a black override darkens the scene. Each control verified its selected material was actually submitted. This establishes missing neutral AO as the cause of that pattern under the controlled setup, independent of the unknown reference environment. The separate constant-white cubemap/flag test also retained the black pattern before this override.

The coordinated uninstalled candidate adds a shared lazy linear white AO texture through `GltfNativeTextureCache` leases to legacy `GltfStockMaterialAdapter.Acquire` only. Authored occlusion goes through `NeedsSourceLit` / `AcquireSource`, which the change does not modify. A source/canonical AO render regression with white and black controls and a cache ownership/failure regression are prepared; neither has been run against the changed producer. Further authored AO strength/UV GPU control is assigned to the NormalTangent owner. Final source/compiled captures and user approval remain pending.
