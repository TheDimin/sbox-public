## Integration proposal, contingent on native tests

Keep template selection explicit and do not turn on the public projected route until normal-map and alpha/variant features satisfy native tests. The candidate uses `GltfManagedModelResource(document, encodedImageBudget, selectTemplate, unlitCapabilities)`, `GltfGpuAsset.PrepareProjected`, physical UV semantics `[0,1,4,5,6]`, `GltfStockMaterialAdapter.PrepareSource/AcquireSource`, and `GltfGpuGeneratedTangentRoute.Prepare`. Use a bounded source-image budget established by integration rather than assuming this model's diagnostic limit applies to all assets.

In `GltfStockMaterialAdapter.AcquireSource`, the material cache key needs `F_RENDER_BACKFACES` derived from the actual prepared double-sided flag; `g_flGltfDoubleSided` must likewise reflect that flag and be checked/bound only in a program variant where it survives reflection, or be replaced by a shader contract whose reflection works for both material variants. Make that variant/reflection contract observable first. Preserve texture roles and independent texture samplers and keep template, scalar/feature state and UV transforms in the cache identity. Removing the double-sided rejection is contingent on asymmetric front/back normal and geometric-normal image proof, including a negative green-channel and handedness control. Shader currently reverses final transformed shading and geometric normals once for a backface; no other layer should reverse them again.

The candidate shader source alone is not acceptance: source and compiled projected draws, TBN, normal scale and selected/transformed UV, reflection, backface, retained material bindings and deletion controls need native evidence. Public `Model.Load` and ordinary `ModelRenderer`, source/compiled current captures, and human reference review must follow integration. The upstream front screenshot uses unknown illumination and camera parameters; it is not an exact pixel oracle. Windows and performance remain open.
