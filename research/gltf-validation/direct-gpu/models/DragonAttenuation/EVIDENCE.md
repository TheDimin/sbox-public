# DragonAttenuation comparison

The previous `DragonAttenuation/glTF` rejection at fingerprint `644c991a9203e63c1cf489278158b2643cad79d24ad0e6bc4848db20902d105b` reports mismatched framing and glass that appears white. The [pinned asset documentation](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/blob/90d7ede14c7e280af263824604b427a1ca02cb66/Models/DragonAttenuation/README.md) identifies the high-resolution reference as a rasterized image under Footprint Court. It shows the checkerboard through refracting glass, with more absorption in the thicker body and less in the horns and claws. The second reference demonstrates the authored Surface Color variant. Both authored materials transmit fully, have zero roughness, and share the same thickness input and implicit IOR 1.5. The Attenuation material has white base color, finite attenuation distance 0.155 m and linear attenuation color (0.921, 0.640, 0.064). The Surface Color variant puts that yellow in the base color and uses the default infinite attenuation distance.

## Verified offline

- The pinned glTF, buffer, two images and two large reference images match the six literal hashes in `../../../../../engine/Tools/Gltf/direct-gpu/models/DragonAttenuation/test_source_oracle.py`. The three Python tests passed. Dragon's 0.25 uniform node scale reduces the local thickness before using the world-space attenuation distance. Two independently decoded thickness texels are green 66 and 242, giving world lengths 0.146882 and 0.538569 m and green transmittances 0.655135 and 0.212103. Omitting node scaling darkens both. The grayscale map cannot distinguish R and G swizzles. Those require the shared synthetic controls with asymmetric channels. [KHR_materials_volume](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF/tree/main/extensions/2.0/Khronos/KHR_materials_volume)
- `../../../../../engine/Tools/Gltf/direct-gpu/models/DragonAttenuation/fit_reference_camera.py` fits a no-roll perspective camera by comparing 32 directional support values of the hue-segmented dragon in the 1101×871 reference with its projected source positions. The fitted camera reduces full-mesh support RMS from 159.04 to 1.10 pixels. The mesh overlay visually aligns the jaws, horn, body arch and feet. This is an inferred fit and an outer-silhouette metric. The original author did not provide camera coordinates, and the native camera image and background framing still require review.
- `../../../../../engine/Tools/Gltf/direct-gpu/models/DragonAttenuation/DragonPublicOpticsTests.cs` compiled against the installed managed reference assemblies with Roslyn. The isolated compiler reported a framework reference-version warning. It did not execute a GPU test. The two native cases require the integrated optical shader and a successful Footprint Court response receipt. They use the public source and one derived canonical compiled form. Each case checks actual draw bindings for both material variants, bound thickness texels after full residency, two GPU index ranges, settled variant and reset captures, and a deletion frame. Shared synthetic framebuffer, refraction, and channel controls remain a separate prerequisite.
