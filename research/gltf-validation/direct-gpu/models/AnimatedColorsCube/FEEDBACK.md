## Pending implementation and acceptance

Shared source is frozen for the integration owner's active benchmark. Proposed limited optional pointer handling carries sampled linear baseColorFactor in immutable per-pose state, keyed by material source index; it must not register extension-wide support or accept unknown required targets. At draw, select the current variant material index, apply the sampled value per draw without mutating cached/shared materials; overrides take precedence. Stock material converts linear RGB to its expected tint encoding. Its shader declares `g_vColorTint` as a material parameter, not an explicitly named RenderAttributes binding; per-draw override behavior and alpha must be proven rather than assumed. A private instance-owned material copy may be required if the stock parameter cannot be overridden, with ownership, override precedence and cleanup tested. Handle default/rest pose and track endpoint, independent phases, reset, disposal and error paths. Explicitly document unimplemented pointer targets.

Native acceptance requires source and canonical compiled input; independent key/intermediate checks of actual GPU positions and the submitted material colour (or rendered colour with a calibrated proof of uniform behavior), wrong-time and wrong-colour controls, two simultaneously visible instances at distinct phases, override/variant and cleanup checks, followed by an actual animation comparison sent to the publication owner.
